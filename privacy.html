Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how Phoenix Insight Studio (“the app”, “we”) handles information. The app is a private tool used only by the Phoenix Insight team to publish our own videos to our own accounts.

1. Who uses the app

Only the Phoenix Insight team. The app is not offered to the public. We do not create accounts for other people and we do not collect information about our viewers.

2. Information the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with our permission, the app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to our channel and to read our channel's name and ID (scopes: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly).
  • TikTok: with our permission (TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API, scopes: user.info.basic, video.upload), the app reads our TikTok account's basic profile (display name, open ID) and uploads our finished videos to our own TikTok inbox. We then review, caption and post each video ourselves in the TikTok app; the app never posts automatically and never reads other users' data.
  • Facebook: the app publishes videos to our own Facebook Page.
  • Video files, scripts and captions that we create ourselves.
  • AI tools: to help write and voice our own scripts, the app sends our script text to Google Gemini (writing and fact-check help) and to ElevenLabs (text-to-speech voice-overs). It sends only our own content, never personal data about other people.

3. How information is used

Only to publish our own videos and to show the connection status inside the app. We do not sell, rent or share any information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

4. Storage and security

Everything stays on our own computer. Access tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain. The app listens only on the computer itself (127.0.0.1); optional phone access is limited to our own private Tailscale network and protected by a PIN. Information is sent only to the service being used (Google/YouTube, Google Gemini, TikTok, Meta, ElevenLabs) over encrypted HTTPS.

5. Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept until we disconnect a platform in the app, which deletes them from the Keychain. Video files are kept on our computer until we delete them.

6. Revoking access

  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions
  • Facebook: Business settings → System users / Integrations

7. Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. TikTok

TikTok's handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not collect information from children.

10. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
